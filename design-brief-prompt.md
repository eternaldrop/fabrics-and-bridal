# Design Brief — Waystream Fabrics & Bridal

Editorial bridal lookbook aesthetic. NOT a generic SaaS product.

## Color Palette (exact hex values)
- Base/background: light cream — `#F7F1E8` (dominant, ~70% of any screen)
- Primary accent: dusty rose pink — `#D9A5AE` (CTAs like "Book Consultation," mood-board swatches, hover states — used deliberately, not as a background)
- Secondary accent: deeper blush — `#C77F8C` (for emphasis where the lighter pink needs more contrast)
- Contrast/anchor: warm near-black — `#1A1714` (headlines, body text, primary buttons, borders — NOT pure #000)
- Neutral support: warm taupe/grey — `#8B8378` (secondary text, captions, dividers)

## Avoid
- Pure black or pure white
- Purple/lavender instead of true pink
- Pink used as a large background
- Generic rounded SaaS cards with grey drop-shadows
- Gradients
- ALL-CAPS labels
- Numbered "01/02/03" markers unless a section is a genuine step sequence

## Typography
- Headline/display font: refined serif with editorial character (e.g. Fraunces) — page titles, product names, mood-board titles
- Body/UI font: clean warm sans-serif (e.g. Inter or General Sans) — body copy, labels, buttons, forms
- Only these two typefaces. Line length under ~80 characters for body text.

## Layout
- Homepage: full-bleed hero photo (real fabric/outfit imagery) with the serif headline overlaid or set beside it — not centered with a generic gradient
- Catalog pages: asymmetric, left-aligned grid — mix of full-width fabric close-ups and smaller outfit thumbnails, not a uniform grid of identical rounded cards
- Bridal consultation flow: one question/section at a time, feels like a guided personal consultation, not a long form
- Mood board page: large tactile color-swatch blocks as the visual centerpiece (pink accent lives here), alongside inspiration images
- Left-aligned text throughout (not centered)
- Minimal border-radius on buttons (2–4px, not pill-shaped)
- Hairline borders in the taupe color rather than soft drop shadows

## Copy Tone
- Warm and personal, like a stylist speaking to the bride — e.g. "Let's find your palette" rather than "Submit consultation request"
- Buttons state exactly what they do: "Book your consultation," "Add to cart," "View mood board"

Apply consistently across every page and component from the first one built — not just the homepage, and not retrofitted later.
